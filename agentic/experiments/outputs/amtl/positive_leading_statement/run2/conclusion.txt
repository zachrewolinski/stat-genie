A binomial logistic regression of AMTL counts (out of observable sockets) controlling for age, sex (prob_male), and tooth class shows that all non-human genera have significantly lower odds of AMTL than Homo sapiens (odds ratios < 1, p < 0.001). Therefore, modern humans exhibit higher AMTL frequencies than Pan, Papio, and Pongo after accounting for these covariates.
