Each participant had both reader_view conditions, enabling a paired comparison of per-participant mean speeds. A paired t-test on log(mean speed) showed no improvement with reader_view (t=0.039, p=0.969), with a near-zero effect size (Cohen’s dz≈0.005) and only ~0.2% average change.
