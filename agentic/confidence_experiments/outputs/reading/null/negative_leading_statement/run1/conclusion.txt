Mean speed with reader view was lower (374.5) than without (406.5); medians 278.2 vs 294.6. Differences were not statistically significant: Welch t-test on raw speed p=0.397, Mann–Whitney p=0.212, and t-test on log-speed p=0.296. Effect sizes were very small and negative (Cohen’s d ≈ −0.08 raw, −0.10 log). A controlled regression on log speed (num_words, page_id, device, age, correct_rate, retake_trial, english_native) with cluster-robust SEs by participant showed a near-zero effect (≈ −0.8%) and non-significance (p=0.91). Overall there is no evidence that Reader View improves reading speed for individuals with dyslexia; if anything, speeds are slightly lower."}
