Robust OLS (HC3) showed age (beta about 0.043 nuts/sec per year, p=6e-7) and sex (males higher by about 0.29 nuts/sec, p=1.9e-5) significantly associated with higher efficiency; R2 about 0.37. Receiving help had a negative coefficient (about -0.18 nuts/sec, p=0.031) but was based on only 7 help cases and lost significance in log-efficiency OLS (p=0.62) and in a Poisson rate model with log(seconds) offset (p=0.081). Thus there is strong evidence that age and sex influence efficiency, and weak or inconsistent evidence that receiving help does.
