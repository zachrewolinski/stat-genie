Mean speed is slightly lower with reader view (323.3) than without (342.3). The difference is not statistically significant in a Welch t-test (p=0.70, Cohen's d=-0.04) or in a paired t-test on within-participant means (n=73 pairs, p=0.63, d=-0.06). A mixed-effects model controlling for page, device, readability, age, gender, and retake with random intercepts for participant also shows a small negative reader-view coefficient (about -23 speed units) with p=0.47. Overall, there is no evidence that reader view improves reading speed for individuals with dyslexia in this dataset."}
