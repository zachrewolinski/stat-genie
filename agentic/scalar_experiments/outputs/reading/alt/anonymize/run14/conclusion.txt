Mean reading speed was 323.3 wpm with Reader View versus 342.3 wpm without it, a difference of −18.9 wpm. A paired t-test on participant means showed no improvement (t = −0.48, p = 0.635). Cluster-robust OLS (by participant) gave the same −18.9 wpm estimate with a wide 95% CI [−96.8, 58.9] and p = 0.633; a mixed-effects model was also non‑significant (p = 0.558). The effect size was essentially zero (Cohen’s d ≈ −0.04). Overall, there is no evidence that Reader View improves reading speed for individuals with dyslexia in this dataset; if anything, the point estimate is slightly lower."}
