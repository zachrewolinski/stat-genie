Use of live bait is strongly associated with higher catch rates (IRR ≈ 4.48, 95% CI 2.33–8.62, p≈7.1e-06) and larger (mean-centered) group size is also strongly associated with higher catch rates (IRR ≈ 3.21 per centered unit, 95% CI 2.70–3.83, p<<0.001).
